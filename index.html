How it works

When you reach the limit

When iOS detects the limit, a reminder covers the app you are using. Delivery may be delayed by the system.

Allow continuing

There is no forced wait. Continue today allows that app until midnight, when its usual daily limit starts again.

Block until tomorrow

The limit screen has no Continue button, and access resets at midnight. You can open HourRich to change, pause, or delete the limit at any time. Changes take effect immediately.

Daily reminders

One of 365 messages appears each day. It stays the same all day and changes at midnight. When enabled, your own reminder appears alongside the daily message.

How time is counted

iOS measures each app's daily usage, including earlier usage today where available. Associated website use may also count. Reminders appear at your limit, not on every app launch.

Screen Time permission is required to apply app limits.

Privacy policy

Updated: November 17, 2026 · SOTEN, LLC.

HourRich requires no account and has no ads. Your selected apps, usage time, and personal reminders are not sent outside your device.

App selections, limits, and reminders are stored locally and shared only with HourRich's Screen Time extensions. App selection uses Apple's opaque tokens.

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier and purchase information to verify purchases, restores, and renewals. HourRich does not receive your card number or Apple Account password. If verification is temporarily unavailable, your last confirmed access is kept.

Purchase information is also used in RevenueCat to analyze sales and retention. It is not linked to your name or email address and is not used for advertising tracking.

You can erase personal text in Your reminder, delete limit rules, or revoke Screen Time access.

Opening a source link visits an external website governed by its own privacy policy. TestFlight diagnostics and feedback follow Apple's settings and terms.

Purchase records are kept for purchase verification, service operation and applicable legal obligations. For questions about access to or deletion of purchase records, contact SOTEN, LLC. using the support form. Do not send payment card details.
